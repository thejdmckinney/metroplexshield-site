## 🌟 EASIEST DEPLOYMENT: Netlify (5 Minutes)

### Step 1: Go to Netlify
1. Visit **netlify.com**
2. Click "Sign up" (use GitHub, Google, or email)

### Step 2: Deploy Your Site
1. Click **"Deploy manually"** or drag & drop
2. **Drag your entire project folder** to the deployment area
3. Wait 30 seconds - **your site is live!**
4. You'll get a URL like `amazing-site-123456.netlify.app`

### Step 3: Test Everything
1. **Click through all pages** (Essential, Guardian, Premier)
2. **Test the contact forms** - they should work immediately!
3. **Try the pricing toggles** (Monthly/Yearly)
4. **Test on your phone**

### Step 4: Connect Your Domain (Optional)
1. Go to **Site settings > Domain management**
2. Click **"Add custom domain"**
3. Enter `metroplexshield.com`
4. Follow DNS instructions from your domain registrar
