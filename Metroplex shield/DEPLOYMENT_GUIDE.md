## 🎯 **Quick Start: Netlify Deployment (Recommended)**

### Step 1: Prepare Your Files
Your website folder should contain:
```
/
├── index.html
├── essential-plan.html
├── guardian-plan.html
├── premier-plan.html
├── sitemap.xml
├── robots.txt
├── assets/
│   ├── css/style.css
│   ├── js/main.js
│   └── images/
│       └── metroplex-shield-logo.svg
```

### Step 2: Update URLs for Production
You'll need to update a few references from localhost to your live domain.

### Step 3: Deploy to Netlify
1. Go to **netlify.com** and sign up
2. Drag your entire website folder to the deployment area
3. Your site will be live at a temporary URL (like `amazing-site-123.netlify.app`)
4. Connect your custom domain in Site Settings > Domain Management
