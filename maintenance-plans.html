Frequently Asked Questions

What's included in your home maintenance plans?

All plans include scheduled preventive maintenance visits, HVAC filter changes, seasonal home inspections, gutter cleaning, priority scheduling, service discounts, and detailed maintenance reports. Every plan is fully customizable to your home's specific needs.

Which plan is best for me?

The Guardian Plan ($149/month) is our most popular choice for busy homeowners who want consistent monthly care without thinking about it. Choose Essential ($89) if you prefer quarterly checkups, or Premier ($249) for maximum peace of mind with bi-weekly visits. We'll help you choose during your free consultation.

Can I customize my plan?

Absolutely! All plans are fully customizable. We can add specific services, adjust visit frequency, or create a completely custom plan based on your home's unique needs and your lifestyle.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contracts required. You can cancel anytime with 30 days notice. We believe in earning your business through exceptional service, not locking you into contracts.

What areas do you serve?

We proudly serve Dallas, Richardson, Plano, Garland, Sachse, Rockwall, University Park, Highland Park, Preston Hollow, and surrounding Dallas-Fort Worth communities.

How do I get started?

Simply book a free consultation using the button below or call us at (682) 466-2130. We'll discuss your home's needs, recommend the best plan, and schedule your first visit.
